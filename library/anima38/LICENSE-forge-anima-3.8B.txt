Not included here:

- Forge's backend/nn/anima.py ``Attention`` (AGPL-3.0). The connector uses the
  trainer's own ``library.anima_models.LLMAdapterAttention`` (same parameter
  names and math, 2-D masks) instead.
- The Qwen3.5-4B tokenizer files (Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B revision
  851bf6e806efd8d0a36b00ddf55e13ccb7b8cd0a, Apache-2.0). They are read from a
  directory at runtime (default: the Forge extension's assets/qwen35_tokenizer),
  not copied, because tokenizer.json is 12.8 MB.
- Model weights (qwen35_4b.safetensors, the Anima-3.8B bundle). They are only
  ever opened read-only.
